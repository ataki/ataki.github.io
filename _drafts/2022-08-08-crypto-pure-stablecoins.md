Vitalik's thought piece is a great starting point on the viability of "crypto-pure" stablecoins. But first, let's recap how we got here.

In 2022, stablecoins rose to the forefront when Terra collapsed. The most salient point was that it wiped out nearly 40B (April high) in market cap. Retail and institutional investors were hurt. Bystanders, not realizing Terra was an experiment, rushed to proclaim crypto-pure stablecoins wouldn't work.

Vitalik's piece was a response to this. But he leaves an open question:

> How do you avoid creating a ponzi scheme with stablecoins while providing a value-add service?

### Learning From Terra

A glimpse of it can be seen with Terra's efforts. Terra's thesis was to ultimately move into payments. Usage and adoption would hlep stabilize the peg. DeFi was the beginning of this wild journey to make a better, decentralized banking system.
